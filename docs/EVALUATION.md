# Evaluation and local pilot

## Deterministic regression corpus

examples/corpus.json contains 60 agent-authored synthetic claims across three topic labels. Thirty explicitly specified pairs cover boolean conflict, repetition, different valid times, definition mismatch, numeric refinement, narrowing/broadening, population mismatch, quotation and overlapping constraints. Labels are not generated by the classifier. Sources are invented; these are regression fixtures, not a human-reviewed real-world gold set.

Current result: 30/30 expected classifications, including nine intended abstentions and zero false contradictions on this fixture. Per-class precision/recall/F1 and the confusion matrix are available through atlas evaluate. The three domain variants share scenarios, so these are not 30 independent semantic challenges.

Extraction quality, human-human agreement and model calibration are null, not zero or invented scores. Review time counts only explicit operator-supplied durations and reports the number of timed reviews.

## Local Castflow pilot

The read-only export on 2026-09-10 selected episodes 2212, 2384, 2434, 2488, 2512, 2569, 2603, 2619, 2644 and 2659 from the main database. The existing snapshot used different episode IDs and was not substituted silently.

Thirty agent-curated candidate claims span ten episodes and seven source labels. Twenty-seven segments have valid timestamps. Three segments from episode 2603 have zero-width timestamps and are explicitly transcript_untimed. No timestamps were invented. Attribution is preserved as provided by Castflow; unnamed speakers remain unattributed.

The evidence, claim candidates, imported document and report are local under data/ and are not checked into Git. The export verifies that the database bytes and modification time did not change while it ran. The manifest records the database digest, selected episode/segment IDs, adapter version and query digest.

All real claims remain unapproved. Unknown scope is explicit, and the deterministic rules abstain instead of turning semantic similarity into contradiction. These 30 candidates are not the source issue's requested 60 human-reviewed claims. Operator validation and a second independent labeling pass remain necessary before claiming that acceptance criterion or a real-world relationship-quality result.

## Interpretation limits

The engine cannot infer sarcasm, source independence, causality or semantic entailment. Mark nonliteral language as unclear/quoted/hypothetical during curation. Incorrect structured inputs can still produce incorrect rule outputs. Human review records attribute decisions; they do not authenticate identities or prove a claim true.
